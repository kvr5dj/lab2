In working to attempt to improve the baseline, I had to consider the results from the first part. As seen, the inner product
method and cosine similarity had the worst results in terms of hitting the right genre. The TFIDF cosine similarity method
had the best results in terms of hitting the right genre. Thus, in doing my method, I considered this with the fact 
that TFIFDF method seemed to do the best in matching relevant songs. Because of this, I combined 2 TFIDF methods, the
TFIDF cosine similarity and TFIDF correlation. In terms of the weighting, I decided to give TFIDF cosine similarity 
more weight (0.8) because cosine similarity seems to do the best. TFIDF correlation got a weight of 0.2. In terms of
my results, I got a hit mean of about 0.24, which is higher than all of the baselines. Thus, I am happy with the results
as this shows my method does slightly improve the baseline.

Thus, while I feel the existing option of the TFIDF cosine similarity worked well, I felt maybe I could leverage the fact
that TFIDF worked really well with the fact the correlation also seems to do well. This led me to why I decided
to combine both TFIDF correlation and TFIDF cosine similarity. This found a way to outperform the available baselines 
because it combined 2 good methods rather than just using 1. This is usually an advantage of using something Like
a weighted average because it can combine 2 methods to make a stronger one.
